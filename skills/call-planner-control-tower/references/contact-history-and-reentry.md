# Previous contact is an eligibility gate

A standing correction from the business owner: a short ask without the previous conversation can burn an account. The planner owns this research; never leave the salesperson to discover it after dialling.

## Before every add, carry-forward or re-prioritisation

1. Resolve the current person ID and company ID. Read the entire relevant note body, not just its title or a `Read note ...` pointer. Reconcile current person and company notes/activity, tasks (complete pagination, at most 10 per page), Call History, the live row's outcome and relevant email/meeting evidence. Use a fresh second queue read before writing.
   - Known defect pattern: live name-cell notes and historical rows can contain another person's ID (one contact's ID attached to other contacts). A stored ID is not verified merely because it appears in a cell note. Match current name, exact email/phone and employer against Attio; hold conflicts. Do not propagate those IDs into deduplication or conversation counts. Preserve evidence and repair only after identity is resolved. When sorting, move complete native rows including notes, formulas, validation and controls; values-only rebuilds can strand provenance on the wrong contact.
2. Keep separate: last attempted call; last two-way conversation with this person; company-level conversations with other people; last outbound email; webinar signal; promised next step; explicit callback; planner due reason. An Attio last-interaction timestamp can be only an email and is not a call count. CRM lifecycle does not prove first contact. A Prospect can have previous calls without proven ICP; a call alone does not authorise MQL/SQL progression.
3. Read the latest outcome and objections. A person replacement, a new owner, a stale open task, a new day, a cleared checkbox or a regenerated queue is not a new call reason. A historical generic cold-call task cannot override a later connected conversation or hold.
4. For a previously contacted person, require an unfulfilled agreed callback, an explicit approved retry, or a materially new, due, value-bearing reason consistent with their latest response. If timing/reason is missing or contradictory, hold for the planner to reconcile. Do not invent a universal cooldown or automatically call again the next business day. A future webinar or future campaign follow-up waits; an independent due callback is considered separately, then merged into one conversation.
5. Missing or conflicting recent history blocks a new recommendation; it is not proof of a fresh prospect or of a customer-requested hold. During a narrow cleanup, do not turn incomplete verification into an invented HOLD label and then use that label to delete an existing call. Reconcile the evidence, preserve human work, and distinguish a confirmed restriction from the planner's uncertainty. No-answer/voicemail is an attempt, not a conversation. Do not repeat an already-worked webinar signal or introduce Daily Practice cold after a known conversation.
6. Show the previous touch's date and useful outcome in H beside the direct ask. Use a verified conversation ordinal only when complete, deduplicated person history supports it. Otherwise `Follow-up` plus the verified last conversation is better than an invented second/third call. Keep detailed provenance and uncertainty in P or the H cell note.
7. Preserve useful missing removal/hold instructions in Attio under current write authority, attached to the correct person/company and with the source and scope explicit. Reuse existing notes/tasks rather than duplicating them. Attio is the planning source; local recovery files and Call History are evidence to reconcile, not a second suppression system. Check current Attio controls on every intake, including alternate contacts. Re-entry requires an explicit release or a recorded condition becoming true; re-check first. Do not clear a hold merely because its review date arrived. Company-scoped holds apply to alternate contacts; person-specific holds do not suppress unrelated people by inference. Priority and ownership decisions stay separate.

## Regression cases

- A company note that describes conversations with two other people, then prioritises a third person, is not proof of a first conversation with that third person. When the salesperson confirms recent conversations, do not call; preserve the removal request. A possible weeks-away revisit is conditional, not an agreed customer callback. Never silently unassign the owner or promote lifecycle.
- A verified recent conversation with a follow-up email sent: the promised-material callback and a later campaign call must be coordinated, not duplicated. Scheduled invitations are neither sent emails nor registrations. Show actual registration/attendance evidence separately from MQL stage.
- A connected checkbox whose prose names somebody else: identity conflict; do not transfer its conversation count to the row's person.
- A person with no person-linked notes but company history: read company notes before deciding. Zero returned person notes is not `first conversation`.
- A recent call plus an old overdue generic task: hold until the task is reconciled with the newer outcome; the overdue flag is not permission to repeat the pitch.

## Scope of a content repair

Narrow Ask edits do not certify the entire queue, trigger closeout, reset checkboxes or execute outcomes. Preserve active human work. Clearly report any audit coverage limit; never claim all call histories are checked when only selected accounts were reconciled.
