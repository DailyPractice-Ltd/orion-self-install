---
name: hire-agent
description: Hire or promote an agent in the client's team. Use when the client says anything like "hire an agent", "I need someone to…", "add someone to my team", "promote {name}", or "{name} should also…". Runs the guided interview, writes the job file, does the agent's first run on real work with the client, wires a schedule if one was chosen, and reports the hire.
---

Read `library/HIRING.md` and follow it exactly — it is the entire procedure and it wins
over anything else you have read about hiring.

Part A is a hire, Part B is a promotion, Part D covers half-done hires and surfaces
that cannot schedule. Do not improvise steps, do not skip the job-sheet readback, and
do not ask the client anything their knowledge base already answers.

An agent is hired once it has done its job on real work and reported it. A schedule
is optional: an agent without one is on call and works when asked.
