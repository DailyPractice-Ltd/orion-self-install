# HubSpot coverage review

Read the shared [HubSpot model](../../lifecycle-governance/references/hubspot-model.md).
Scope the review to the current request and Henri's verified owner; don't silently
claim teammates' records. For a broad inventory, paginate all in-scope COMPANY and
CONTACT records separately. Reconcile companies, associated contacts, lifecycle,
owner, domain, tasks, relevant history and current queue coverage. Keep missing
lifecycle/owner explicit. A blank field is unknown, not Prospect or unassigned permission.

Report company totals and contact totals separately, with filters, pagination,
snapshot date and coverage limits. A company-level stage does not establish a
person's qualification. If only a sample is needed or possible, label it sampled;
never extrapolate full coverage or turn access failures into zero counts.

Present observed target options and actionable gaps with verified HubSpot links,
current owner/stage, role/sector evidence and callable coverage. Propose the best
supported next step; no invented conversion scores, employee thresholds or quotas.
For a broad comparison a chart can help; a narrow supplied list does not require
an unrelated full-portal graph. Avoid repeating captured ICP questions.

Deliver the review in conversation. There is no standing Slack #prospecting
publication for this installation. If Henri requests a share, stage the exact
content and verified destination, obtain approval, then publish and read it back.
CRM corrections, assignment, merges and queue writes need their own exact approval.
