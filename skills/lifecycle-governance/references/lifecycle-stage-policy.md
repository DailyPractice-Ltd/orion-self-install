# HubSpot lifecycle evidence policy

Resolve object and property using [HubSpot model](hubspot-model.md). These are
conservative gates for proposals; schema labels do not prove that a record passed them.

| Proposed classification | Required evidence | Insufficient evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect | Verified identity in the requested prospecting scope | A guessed name or employer |
| Lead | Current ICP/qualification evidence | Title or company size alone |
| MQL | Documented marketing qualification/handoff for that contact/account | Source tag, imported stage or registration alone |
| SQL | Accepted qualified meeting and evidence of qualification | Suggested date, voicemail or task |
| Opportunity lifecycle | Actual associated commercial opportunity | Proposal idea or pricing mentioned |
| Customer | Verified purchase/contract and payment evidence from the real process | Promise to pay, draft proposal or a lifecycle label |
| Closed/Lost lifecycle or Closed Lost deal | Prior SQL-or-later evidence and explicit loss outcome on the specific opportunity | Silence, wrong number, missing mobile or unqualified cold prospect |

Evangelist and Subscriber are configured options, not automatic next steps. No
Blocked or Signed trial lifecycle option was found; leave holds in verified existing
controls. CS - Pilot is a deal-stage label, not proof of a signed trial.

When considering loss, inspect other deals and existing customer status first.
Closing one deal never automatically closes all contacts or the company. If no
stage history is available, do not infer the highest stage from numeric/display order.
For incorrect pre-SQL loss, propose the last proven valid lifecycle, or explain why
Prospect is a suggested fallback. Apply only the approved correction with readback.
Preserve owner, unrelated fields, associations, tasks and activity history.
