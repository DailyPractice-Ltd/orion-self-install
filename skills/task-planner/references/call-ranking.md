# HubSpot call readiness and ranking

Read the current task's full meaning and associations. A CALL task can still be
held by a dependency; a TODO describing a real call may qualify when evidenced.
Email, phone-field repair, identity research and admin tasks are not calls.
Use NOT_STARTED/IN_PROGRESS due actions as candidates; reconcile WAITING/DEFERRED
conditions and exclude COMPLETED. Do not assume the first search page is exhaustive.

Require current contact identity/employer, aligned owner, allowed verified route,
a genuine due call reason, no explicit hold/DNC and no newer contradictory outcome.
Apply the [history gate](../../call-planner-control-tower/references/contact-history-and-reentry.md).
A company stage alone does not classify a contact. Use actual callback time first,
then due time and current engagement; labels P0/P1/P2 are planning priorities, not CRM
stage mutations. P0 needs an urgent evidenced booking/decision objective, not merely
MQL or customer status. Never invent MRR or authority to improve ranking.

For pre-SQL calls the usual objective is booking a later 20-minute Google Meet demo.
Preserve more specific next steps and previous conversation context. Keep task ID,
contact/company IDs and evidence date in supporting notes. Due date is not a promised
callback. Prefer verified direct routes; label reception as such.

Taskless research candidates follow Prospect Call List; webinar candidates follow
the conditional webinar reference. An expired engagement boost never removes a
useful existing row. Morning planning changes neither CRM status nor human controls.
