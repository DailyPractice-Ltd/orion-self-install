# Optional webinar follow-up

Use only for a webinar cohort included in the request and supported by accessible
sources. Ordinary due HubSpot call tasks do not require three emails or a webinar.
Read the exact associated task, contact, current employer, owner, lifecycle, history,
holds and event dependencies. An earlier event's due task need not wait for a later
webinar. A scheduled invitation is not a sent email, registration or attendance.

Match exact recipient email and event identity. Registration proves signup only;
attendance needs attendance evidence, and recording signup does not prove viewing.
If three-email completion matters, verify three distinct actual sends with IDs and
timestamps for that person and campaign. SENT alone does not establish delivery.
Read the relevant complete cohort; missing access means unknown, not unregistered.

Preserve actual callbacks and latest outcomes. For taskless follow-up, stage a
proposed timing with its evidence; no universal next-business-day or 1–5-day priority
policy is assumed for Henri. Use such timing only when the current brief establishes
it. Resolve timezone/working-day uncertainty when it affects a proposed date.
Engagement does not automatically change lifecycle or create SQL.

Deduplicate by contact ID and event/source key against current tasks, queue and
history. Show verified webinar signal and last conversation beside the demo-booking
objective, with source IDs in notes. Keep pending invitations distinct from signup.
Stage queue changes; never create or reschedule tasks, send emails, enrol sequences,
spend credits or publish a Slack graph during intake.
