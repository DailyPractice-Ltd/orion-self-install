# Prospect Research → Outreach

1. Identify the prospect from the name, company, profile, or links provided. Check
   CRM history and find verifiable facts. Summarise who they are, why they fit the
   ICP, and what specific fact gives you a reason to reach out.
2. Draft outreach in the client's voice using that fact. If another skill asks for
   drafting only, return the draft to it here. For a full run, follow rule 1 in the
   agent definition to score the message and decide whether to send it or leave it
   waiting. Keep a waiting message in the client's own Drafts if the email connector
   is live, or show it in the conversation and say where it is.
3. Apply the agent definition's CRM rule. Record the prospect and the next action,
   including the source channel, draft status, and actual last touch. Use the CRM's
   Prospect stage for a new prospect. Report a completed write in one line.

Use the core connector setup for email and CRM. Verify the full run with VT-01 in
`validation/validation-tasks.md` on a real prospect. Check the CRM round-trip in VT-03.
