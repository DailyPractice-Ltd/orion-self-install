# Attio plans the calls; the queue is the worklist

The business owner's standing correction supersedes any rejected backlog workflow. Attio is the source of truth for account history, next actions, timing, holds and suppression. Do not create a Planner Backlog or another shadow CRM.

## Cleanup is not a fresh intake

When asked to remove wait/do-not-call cases, remove only rows with an explicit, evidenced wait, hold, skip, removal or do-not-call instruction. Preserve the other existing useful calls. An internally generated planning slot, absence of a due-today task, a checked Dialled box or incomplete re-verification is not by itself a removal reason. Do not reinterpret a focused cleanup as permission to empty or rebuild the queue.

Respect actual customer-agreed timing, explicit task/event dependencies and current suppression. Distinguish these from internal capacity slots. New task-derived intake still follows Task Planner's due/overdue rules; new research prospects and webinar signals follow their own approved intake routes. A useful prospect call does not require manufacturing an Attio task.

## Preserve work and context

Read current Attio and the live row before removal. Under the current request's authority, reconcile useful missing factual context or an explicit user planning instruction into the correct existing Attio person/company record or task. Reuse evidence already in Attio; never bulk-copy generic asks, stale personalisation, inferred callback times or unverified outcomes. A planning-control note is not a connected-call log. Read back any CRM write.

Preserve user outcomes, links and checkboxes during restoration or cleanup. Do not execute instructions or clear/reset controls as part of a placement repair. Actual completed calling outcomes are reconciled under the separately authorised closeout process; Call History is an evidence ledger, not an alternative planning source.

Record source IDs so a separately authorised closeout can find an unresolved instruction in Attio and avoid duplicate execution. Keep future calls and holds in Attio, not in a new sheet. Missing write authority is a specific blocker to resolve, not permission to invent another worklist.

## Daily planning

Scope from current Attio companies, people, full note bodies, tasks and current communication/webinar evidence. Read the live queue and Call History to preserve human work and avoid duplicates. Admit call-now/today cases; do not recycle a prior attempt or override a real hold. Never infer untouched status from Prospect lifecycle.

This is a placement and source-of-truth rule, not permission for calls, sends, lifecycle/ownership changes, task rescheduling, new schedules or unattended go-live.
