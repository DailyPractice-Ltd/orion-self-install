# Sheet stage evidence

Use the spreadsheet's live stage list as the allowed labels. Read the complete sheet history before changing a stage. A new label requires evidence for that exact row; retain the current value and report a conflict when evidence is incomplete. The examples below apply only when those labels exist in the live list.

| Stage | Evidence required in the spreadsheet |
| --- | --- |
| Prospect | Verified person or company within the requested prospecting scope. |
| Lead | Current qualification fit supported beyond title or company size. |
| MQL | Documented marketing qualification and handoff. Registration alone is insufficient. |
| SQL | Accepted qualified meeting and documented need, budget and authority, or the user's recorded equivalent. |
| Deals (Opportunity) | An actual commercial opportunity recorded in the sheet history. A proposal or price mention alone is insufficient. |
| Customer | Verified purchase or contract and payment evidence recorded in the sheet history. |
| Evangelist | Assignment criteria must be supplied by the user. Hold until then. |
| Closed/Lost | A specific opportunity with prior SQL-or-later evidence and explicit loss recorded in the sheet history. Silence, an unreachable prospect, a rejection before opportunity, or “close the prospect” alone is insufficient. Preserve earlier customer history and check for other active opportunities in the sheet. |
| Subscriber | Assignment criteria must be supplied by the user. Hold until then. |
